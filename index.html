THE FLOW

Three steps to control what goes out.

1. It scans on your machine.

Your query and any documents you attach are scanned locally for names, companies, ID numbers, addresses and phone numbers. Nothing has left yet.

2. You review what's leaving.

You see your document beside the version the model will receive, line for line. Change what's masked, mask something we missed, or unmask something that isn't sensitive. Nothing is sent until you say so.

3. Only the masked version goes out.

The AI works with pseudonyms — Person A, Company Alpha. When the answer comes back, we put the real names back in on your machine. You read it normally. The model never saw them.

PRIVACY, BY DESIGN

What we do, and what we don't.

01

What stays on your machine

Entity detection, your documents, your conversation history, and the mapping between real names and pseudonyms. All of it is stored locally. We don't have a copy.

02

What gets sent

The masked text of your query and documents, to the AI model you choose. Legal language, dates and amounts go through as written — the model can't reason about your matter otherwise. That's a deliberate trade-off, and it's worth understanding before you rely on this.

03

What we can't promise

Automated masking isn't the same as legal-grade redaction. Detection is good, not perfect, which is exactly why we show you everything before it's sent and ask you to approve it. A careful read of that screen is part of using this properly.

04

If the local scanner fails

Privi keeps working with pattern matching alone and tells you clearly that detection is reduced. It won't quietly send unmasked text.
